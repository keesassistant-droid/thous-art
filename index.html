About Me

thous-art paints in bold, saturated colour. Places and moments rendered so they feel warmer and louder than memory usually allows. Every piece starts as a conversation before it becomes a brushstroke.

Based wherever the current commission takes it, thous-art works across sizes and surfaces, from small studies to statement pieces made for a specific wall.
